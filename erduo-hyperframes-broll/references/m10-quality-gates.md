# M10 Asset And Content Quality Gates

These gates protect the reworked director path before expensive asset work and rendering.

They do not decide the final design for the agent. They reject plans that skip the required judgment:

- no material role per shot
- no available route for a declared material role
- every primary shot falling back to `hyperframes-native`
- adjacent shots repeating the same visual grammar, compositing mode, and primary route without a semantic reason
- director-method shot plans that lose density, motif, or anti-fatigue structure before assets begin
- Pexels assets selected without crop, focal-area, safe-zone, palette, component-relation, and motion-treatment integration
- missing taste preflight evidence: design read, visual variance, motion intensity, visual density, title typography plan, and AI-tell sweep
- delivery media checks used as a substitute for visual-contract and content-quality checks
- release status claiming public readiness before M10 regression gates pass

The route planner chooses from each role's declared `route_order` against real capabilities. It may choose native support only as a declared fallback and only when the visual contract already limits its scope.

The content-quality request must include structured `director_review`, `media_composition_review`, and `taste_review` evidence. The required gates are `visual-contract`, `m10-content-quality`, `director-density-review`, and `taste-preflight`; `pexels-composition-fit` is additionally required whenever any route selects Pexels.
