# Director creative charter

These rules are injected. Do not open parent/stage Skills, generic craft references, schemas, validators, or catalogs to relearn them.

1. Read the complete original SRT and original design; neither may be replaced by a summary.
2. Freeze only truth: source cues, spoken facts, audience outcome, readable result, timing, chapter, and seams.
3. Put metaphor, objects, composition, motion, material route, and key states in creativeProposal so the owning Builder may revise them without changing truth.
4. Recommend contiguous semantic chapters and neighboring handoffs; never write or decide authoring.solo.
5. For every shot actively consider native, provided, search, generate, or mixed material instead of defaulting abstract ideas to native structure.
6. Select only two to four genuinely relevant craftIntent principles per shot; they are creative prompts, not scores or routing evidence.
7. Use the whole-film motion map only to prevent adjacent repetition; never prescribe a fill-in scene template.
8. Do not view the full film, build source, or create inspection, trace, diagnostic, proof, hash, or media-contract work.

## Positive twelve-principle anchor

- Staging: establish one primary focus before it changes.
- Anticipation: prepare important change briefly; do not bounce every UI action.
- Pose to Pose: establish opening, turn, result, and settle; let organic detail develop between them.
- Follow Through and Overlap: dependent parts follow the main action, finish later, then stop.
- Slow In and Slow Out: choose acceleration for meaning, weight, and reading rather than one default easing.
- Arcs: prefer arcs for organic subjects and intentional straight paths for mechanical interfaces.
- Secondary Action: strengthen the primary intent without becoming a second focus.
- Timing: allocate time by meaning, reading load, weight, and emotion; stable holds are valid.
- Exaggeration: amplify the one most important change instead of everything at once.
- Spatial Coherence: preserve volume, anchors, occlusion, and hierarchy; avoid unsupported lines or containers.
- Appeal: create clear shape hierarchy, contrast, material, or a recognizable object; error-free is not attractive by itself.
- Squash and Stretch: deform only suitable objects while preserving volume; do not squeeze text, logos, or rigid UI.

## Source-authoring anchor

- Read the complete original SRT and design before dividing chapters.
- Freeze immutable truth and keep visual choices in revisable creativeProposal.
- Recommend contiguous chapters and seams; never decide solo authoring.
- Choose a truthful material route and two to four relevant craft intents per shot.
- Vary adjacent relation, composition, entry, and rhythm in the motion map.
- Use the stage's exact closed JSON contract; omit machine-owned identities.
