# Visual evidence contract

## Pre-render supplemental still facts

Each authoring chunk may freeze source-bound target-raster `entry/result/exit`
facts under the [pre-master contract](pre-master-visual-preflight-contract.md).
They remain private and are invalidated by any source, asset, font, timing or
render-setting change. Current style evidence additionally binds actual compact
shared directive and scoped recipes, block/source/shot/phase, projected frame
and timestamp, projection, renderer/capture receipt, encoded and decoded
hashes, and one review generation. Locators and every ancestor must be
non-symlink regular paths.

The deterministic pixel gate inspects every shot and adjacent result pair,
aggregates all failures and blocks black/empty, bright low-information,
dominant-flat-color, huge-dead-zone, unreadable or repeated output. Its output
is facts plus failure codes only; it cannot carry aesthetic approval, a
quality score or a visual verdict. Hashes, DOM, labels and producer prose never
approve visual quality.

Before integration, the qualified main agent resolves every current block
source/capture/facts generation and issues static-only
`style_conformance_review`. It may judge identity/anti-identity, attention
geometry, subject/title relationship, real HTML/SVG text, accent load,
material/texture, responsible negative space and adjacent result sameness. It
cannot approve animation, rhythm, transitions, lifecycle or seek. After
integration, the main agent separately resolves and reads every actual-source
page under the [main-review packet contract](main-review-packet-contract.md)
and issues `source_code_review`.

## Final-master evidence

`broll-render` may retain master-bound three-state evidence for technical
traceability and deterministic verify. It cannot convert that evidence into a
visual decision. No `final_frame_review` is required or allowed on the active
topology; post-render work is limited to automatic technical integrity.

One aggregate revision is allowed per failed chunk. No independent
visual-review child or serial micro-fix chain belongs to the default runtime.
