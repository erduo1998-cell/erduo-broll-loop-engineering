# Lead creative charter

These rules are injected. Do not open parent/stage Skills, generic craft references, schemas, validators, or catalogs to relearn them.

1. Read the complete original SRT, original design, compact motion map, three representative Recipes, and shared asset/font index directly.
2. Build three genuinely different final samples: native graphic/type, real-or-generated material fusion, and information-dense interface/process/data organization.
3. Implement every signature motion named by the design in a real sample or runnable shared primitive; renaming a generic easing does not count.
4. Deliver reusable crop, mask, depth, background-fusion, provenance-binding, preparation, primary-action, overlap, settle, and readable-hold capabilities.
5. Keep the capability index under one page and say which content relationship each capability serves; never provide a swap-the-copy whole-shot template.
6. Open the three real sample sheets and short previews; revise low-level defects and return one accepted or revised viewing conclusion.
7. Approved samples become the final source for those shots and must not be rebuilt by a later Chapter Builder.
8. Expose every assigned HyperFrames target as one regular source file at compositions/<target.id>.html under sourceRoot; a root-level target HTML file is not bound.
9. Do not create inspection source, DOM markers, trace metadata, motion windows, passing diagnostics, or any capture, proof, hash, probe, decode, manifest, contract, or receipt tool.

## Positive twelve-principle anchor

- Staging: establish one primary focus before it changes.
- Anticipation: prepare important change briefly; do not bounce every UI action.
- Pose to Pose: establish opening, turn, result, and settle; let organic detail develop between them.
- Follow Through and Overlap: dependent parts follow the main action, finish later, then stop.
- Slow In and Slow Out: choose acceleration for meaning, weight, and reading rather than one default easing.
- Arcs: prefer arcs for organic subjects and intentional straight paths for mechanical interfaces.
- Secondary Action: strengthen the primary intent without becoming a second focus.
- Timing: allocate time by meaning, reading load, weight, and emotion; stable holds are valid.
- Exaggeration: amplify the one most important change instead of everything at once.
- Spatial Coherence: preserve volume, anchors, occlusion, and hierarchy; avoid unsupported lines or containers.
- Appeal: create clear shape hierarchy, contrast, material, or a recognizable object; error-free is not attractive by itself.
- Squash and Stretch: deform only suitable objects while preserving volume; do not squeeze text, logos, or rigid UI.

## Source-authoring anchor

- Read the complete original SRT/design and compare representatives with the motion map.
- Build native, material-fusion, and information-dense samples with distinct silhouettes and primary actions.
- Implement named signature motion and reusable material/motion capabilities, never a whole-shot template.
- Bind every assigned target at compositions/<target.id>.html before running the standard command.
- Open the real sheets and short previews, repair visible defects, then record accepted or revised.
- Preserve each sample as final source for its assigned shot.
- Run only the assignment's standard command; Parent owns mechanical media evidence.
