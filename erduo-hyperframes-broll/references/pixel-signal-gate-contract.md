# Pixel signal gate contract

`pixel-signal-gate` runs per block after runtime seek. Its receipt phase is
`block`.

Temporary technical frames may be captured privately to calculate:

- black, near-empty, fully transparent or abnormally frozen output;
- NaN/Infinity transform, DOM/text overflow and clipping;
- non-decorative text overlap and safe-region violations;
- visible primary focal ROI and enabled/disabled material contribution;
- role-bound functional text minimums, actual font/weight/glyph and fallback;
- stable-result presence during the declared hold;
- extreme adjacent-result identity where the contract declares change.

Microtext can be smaller than functional body text, but cannot carry the only
result. Thresholds come from the current validation policy and project
profile; Deep Current ratios are not public defaults.

Frames are deleted or retained only in the private artifact store. They never
enter the parent context, a contact sheet or a visual model. The gate cannot
emit aesthetic scores, “premium” labels or any subjective verdict. Ratio
thresholds are normalized to `[0, 1]`, luma is bounded to `[0, 255]`, and every
receipt code belongs to the `pixel-signal-gate` policy registry.
